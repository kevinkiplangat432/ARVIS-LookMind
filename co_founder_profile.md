# Co-Founder Profile: Legal, Regulatory & Compliance Lead (ARVIS)

## Role Overview

The **Legal & Compliance Co-Founder** will co-own the strategic direction of **ARVIS**, transforming legal and regulatory hurdles into a core market advantage.

This role bridges the gap between deep technical infrastructure, including Go binaries, local databases, and on-premise deployments, and the rigid regulatory perimeters of African financial institutions.

The primary mandate is to:

- Protect the company's intellectual property (IP)
- De-risk enterprise buyers
- Architect the compliance framework that enables Tier 1 banks and fintechs to adopt ARVIS at scale

---

## Core Responsibilities

### 1. Enterprise Procurement Unblocking

Architect and defend the legal and compliance frameworks required to navigate high-stakes enterprise sales cycles with banking CISOs, procurement teams, and legal departments.

Key responsibilities include:

- Vendor Risk Assessment (VRA) frameworks
- Data Processing Addendums (DPAs)
- On-Premise Software Licensing Agreements
- Enterprise procurement and security questionnaires
- Contract negotiation with financial institutions

---

### 2. Regulatory Framework Mapping

Maintain absolute alignment between the ARVIS product roadmap and evolving African regulatory frameworks.

Primary focus areas include:

- **Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (DPA)**
- **Central Bank of Kenya (CBK) Cybersecurity Guidance Notes**
- Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) requirements
- Emerging AI governance and digital financial-services regulations

The role should continuously translate regulatory requirements into actionable product and engineering requirements.

---

### 3. Liability Architecture

Formulate **"Liability Firewalls"** within enterprise contracts.

ARVIS's native on-premise architecture should be leveraged to legally and contractually isolate the company from data-perimeter liabilities belonging to purchasing financial institutions.

This includes defining:

- Data ownership
- Data processing responsibilities
- Security responsibilities
- Incident and breach liability
- Audit obligations
- Indemnification boundaries
- Deployment-specific responsibilities
- Customer versus ARVIS operational responsibilities

---

### 4. Corporate Governance

Manage the company's legal and regulatory positioning as ARVIS scales.

Responsibilities include:

- Regulatory engagement with the **Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC)**
- Corporate structuring
- Potential offshore structures and Delaware flips
- Investor legal mechanics
- SAFE agreements
- Priced equity rounds
- Founder and shareholder agreements
- Intellectual property protection
- Corporate compliance

---

## Infrastructure Literacy

The candidate should possess a **strong conceptual understanding of enterprise software architecture**.

They should comfortably understand the compliance and liability differences between:

> **External cloud-hosted SaaS**

and

> **Localized, zero-export on-premise utility infrastructure**

The ideal candidate does not need to be an engineer, but should be able to understand concepts such as:

- On-premise deployments
- Reverse proxies
- Local data processing
- Data residency
- Zero-export architectures
- API integrations
- Enterprise network boundaries
- Encryption
- Identity and access controls
- Audit logging
- Data minimization

Most importantly, they should understand how technical architecture translates into **legal risk allocation**.

# What Success Looks Like

The Legal, Regulatory & Compliance Co-Founder should ultimately help make ARVIS **easier for regulated enterprises to buy**.

Their work should turn:

**Regulatory complexity → Product advantage**

**Enterprise legal friction → Faster procurement**

**Data-residency concerns → Architectural differentiation**

**Liability concerns → Clearly defined contractual boundaries**

**African regulatory fragmentation → A scalable compliance framework**

The goal is not simply to keep ARVIS legally compliant.

The goal is to make **compliance itself part of ARVIS's competitive moat.**
