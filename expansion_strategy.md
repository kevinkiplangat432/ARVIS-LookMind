### the strategy
ARVIS Channel Partner & Systems Integrator (SI) Strategy
Objective To decouple ARVIS's geographical and revenue expansion from linear engineering headcount growth by leveraging Tier-1 global advisory firms (e.g., Deloitte, PwC, EY) as primary distribution and deployment vectors.
Partner Value PropositionNew Service Lines: Partners can attach high-margin implementation, configuration, and continuous AI auditing services to the ARVIS platform.Instant Regulatory 

Alignment: Partners gain a turn-key, on-premise technological solution to validate and enforce their clients' compliance with the Kenyan Data Protection Act (DPA 2019) and regional AI mandates.

Team: Enterprise client lead generation, localized procurement management, Tier-1/Tier-2 front-line deployment, and user training.Commercial Incentive StructureSoftware
